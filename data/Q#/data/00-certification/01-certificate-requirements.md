# PolyCode Q# Certificate — Requirements

Earn the **PolyCode Q# Developer Certificate** by completing this course and demonstrating practical skills.

## Requirements

### 1. Complete all modules

| Module | Lessons |
|--------|---------|
| 01 Beginner | 6 |
| 02 Intermediate | 6 |
| 03 Advanced | 6 |
| 04 Professional | 6 |
| 05 Mastery | 6 |

### 2. Hands-on projects

Complete **at least 3 projects** across the course, including a tested algorithm implementation, a resource-estimation or hybrid-workflow report, and the mastery capstone.

### 3. Final assessment

- Score **80% or higher** on the [final assessment](02-final-assessment.md)
- Review weak topics before retaking

## How to submit

Track progress in your PolyCode profile and complete the assessment when ready.
